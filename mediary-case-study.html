Project Overview

Mediary is a cutting-edge healthcare application designed to transform how medical professionals conduct and document patient consultations. By leveraging advanced AI voice recognition technology, Mediary eliminates the burden of manual note-taking, allowing doctors to focus entirely on patient care while the system automatically transcribes, organizes, and structures medical encounters.

The platform addresses one of healthcare's most persistent challenges: the administrative overhead that takes doctors away from patient interaction. With Mediary, physicians can conduct natural conversations with patients while the AI intelligently captures symptoms, diagnoses, treatment plans, and follow-up instructions in real-time.

Mobile interface showcasing seamless patient management

Problem & Solution

The Problem

Medical professionals spend 30-50% of their consultation time on documentation rather than patient care. Manual note-taking leads to incomplete records, transcription errors, and physician burnout. Traditional EHR systems are clunky and interrupt the natural flow of doctor-patient communication.

The Solution

Mediary uses AI-powered voice recognition to automatically transcribe and structure medical consultations. The system intelligently categorizes information into relevant medical sections, generates structured SOAP notes, and integrates seamlessly with existing healthcare workflows, reducing documentation time by 80%.

Impact

Doctors save an average of 2 hours per day on documentation, allowing more time for patient care. Medical records are more comprehensive and accurate. Patient satisfaction increased by 35% as physicians maintain better eye contact and engagement during consultations.

Innovation

First-of-its-kind medical AI that understands context, medical terminology, and patient narratives. The system learns from each doctor's preferences and adapts its documentation style accordingly, creating personalized, efficient workflows.

Key Features

🎙️ Real-Time Voice Transcription

Advanced speech-to-text engine specifically trained on medical terminology and conversations, capturing every detail with 98% accuracy.

📋 Intelligent Documentation

Automatically structures consultation notes into SOAP format (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan), ready for EMR integration.

🔐 HIPAA Compliant

End-to-end encryption and strict privacy controls ensure patient data is always protected and compliant with healthcare regulations.

📊 Patient History

Quick access to previous consultations, medications, and treatment history, providing complete context for every encounter.

🔄 EMR Integration

Seamless integration with major Electronic Medical Record systems, eliminating duplicate data entry and streamlining workflows.

📱 Multi-Platform

Available on iOS, Android, and web, ensuring doctors can access patient information and document encounters from any device.

📈 E&M Coding Assistant

Intelligent E&M (Evaluation and Management) coding system that automatically calculates the appropriate billing level based on medical decision-making complexity, data reviewed, and risk assessment. The AI analyzes consultation content to suggest accurate CPT codes, ensuring proper reimbursement while maintaining compliance.

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

The wireframing phase was crucial in establishing the foundation of Mediary's user experience. We started with low-fidelity wireframes to rapidly explore different layouts, user flows, and interaction patterns without getting caught up in visual details. This iterative approach allowed us to test multiple concepts with actual physicians and receive feedback on functionality before committing to high-fidelity designs.

Working closely with medical professionals during this phase helped us understand their real needs and pain points. We discovered that simplicity was key—doctors needed to access critical features with minimal taps, and the interface had to stay out of their way during patient interactions. These insights directly shaped the final product architecture.

Email Campaign Design

As part of Mediary's marketing strategy, I designed a comprehensive email campaign system to engage medical professionals and increase product adoption. The email designs prioritize clarity, professionalism, and mobile responsiveness while maintaining brand consistency across all touchpoints.

Each email template was crafted with UX writing principles in mind—using concise, benefit-driven copy, clear calls-to-action, and scannable layouts that respect the busy schedules of healthcare professionals. The design system includes onboarding sequences, feature announcements, educational content, and engagement campaigns.

Key design considerations included accessibility, HIPAA-compliant messaging, and optimization for both desktop and mobile email clients. The templates incorporate medical imagery, data visualizations, and testimonials to build trust and demonstrate value.
