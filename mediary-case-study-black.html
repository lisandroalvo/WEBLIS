Case Study

MEDIARY

AI-Powered Note-Taker for Healthcare Professionals

Role
Senior UI/UX Designer
Industry
Healthcare AI
Timeline
Jun 2024 - Nov 2025
Platform
Web & Mobile

Project Overview

Mediary is an innovative AI-powered platform designed to revolutionize clinical documentation for healthcare professionals. The system uses advanced speech recognition and natural language processing to automatically transcribe and structure doctor-patient conversations into comprehensive medical notes.

As the Senior UI/UX Designer, I led the design strategy, creating an intuitive interface that prioritizes speed, accuracy, and HIPAA compliance while reducing documentation time by up to 70%.

The Challenge

Time Constraints

Doctors spend 2-3 hours daily on documentation, reducing patient care time and contributing to burnout.

Privacy & Security

Medical records require strict HIPAA compliance and secure data handling for patient privacy protection.

Accuracy Requirements

Clinical documentation must be precise, comprehensive, and formatted according to medical standards.

The Solution

We designed a streamlined workflow that allows doctors to focus on patient care while AI handles the documentation. The interface features real-time transcription, smart templates, and one-click note generation.

Real-Time Transcription

Live audio-to-text conversion with medical terminology recognition and automatic formatting.

Smart Templates

Customizable SOAP note templates that adapt to different specialties and documentation needs.

Seamless Integration

Direct integration with major EHR systems for one-click export and minimal workflow disruption.

Impact & Results

70% Time Saved

Average documentation time reduced from 15 minutes to under 5 minutes per patient encounter.

95% Accuracy

AI-powered transcription achieves 95% accuracy on medical terminology with continuous improvement.

500+ Doctors

Successfully onboarded across multiple healthcare facilities with 4.8/5 satisfaction rating.
